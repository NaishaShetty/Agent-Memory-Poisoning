## Reading This Matrix

- The **High-compatibility / Faithful-fidelity** cells cluster around SENTINEL (D1)
  and A-MemGuard (D3/D4-adjacent) — these are the two components worth prototyping
  as faithfully-reproduced, separately-ablatable pieces of a larger design, not as
  competing "pick one" baselines.
- Every other row is either weak-by-its-own-authors'-evidence (ASB, AgentPoison's own
  defenses, MINJA's own defenses) or unvalidated-by-its-own-authors (MemoryGraft
  reranking) or provably narrow (MEMSAD's paraphrase blind spot).
- **No row covers D2** (a persistent, propagating trust/provenance state). This
  absence is the clearest, most consistent finding across the entire literature
  reviewed — see `DEFENSE_GAP_ANALYSIS.md`.
