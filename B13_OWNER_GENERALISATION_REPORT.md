## 1. A correction to the previous round (confirmed defect)

`FINAL_GAP_CLOSURE_REPORT.md`, `CALIBRATION_AND_LEAKAGE_AUDIT.md` and T15 reported that *"B13 thresholds move ≈3× with the calibration owners; benign exclusion moves from 12–15% to 29–35%"*. **That is withdrawn. It compared two benign budgets, not two owner sets.**

**What happened:**

1. B13's frozen constants (`SUPPORT_EXCLUDE = 0.0019`, `SUPPORT_FLAG = 0.0057`) were set in round 4. They were the 2% and 5% quantiles of the 1,026 dev observations, with `SUPPORT_TOP_K = 5`.
2. Round 6 (commit `8cd3bfc5`) widened retrieval to `SUPPORT_TOP_K = 20` and kept the constants.
3. At top-20, the constants sit at the **0.49% / 2.05%** quantiles of the same observations (5 and 21 of 1,026).
4. The "disjoint" arm of `corroboration_robustness.py` re-ran the *nominal* 2%/5% rule at top-20 on owners {2, 4}. The result was 0.00555 / 0.01594.

**Check.** The same rule on the *original* owners {1, 2, 3, 4} at top-20 gives 0.00555 / 0.01574. That is the same threshold, so the ≈3× was a different budget, not a different set of owners.

**Regression test.** `test_shipped_constants_sit_at_half_percent_budget_at_top20`. T15 now carries this correction.

## 2. Protocol (as pre-registered)

**Units: the 10 LoCoMo owners.** All ten are eligible. Each owner has:

| Population | Count per owner | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Benign derived memories | 169–324 | The dataset's own `observation` memories, through the untrusted `agent_generated` channel. 2,541 in total |
| Fabrications | 20 | 12 additive and 8 preference. 200 in total |

The fabrications were generated by **qwen2.5:7b for owners 1–4** and **mistral:7b for owners 0 and 5–9**. That is a confound between split and generator, so results are reported per split as well.

No owner lacks a category.

**Calibration.**
- The procedure is B13's own: quantiles of benign-observation support (NLI entailment, top-20 premises, the owner's own conversation), pooled over the calibration owners.
- For each held-out owner *o*, calibration uses **the other nine owners' observations only**. The thresholds are frozen and written out before *o* is scored, and *o* is scored once.
- Nothing about *o* influences:
  - the quantile level;
  - the decision rule;
  - any policy constant.

  This is checked by `test_loo_calibration_never_contains_the_held_out_owner`.

**Operating points (all pre-declared).**

| Point | Definition | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **A-ship** (primary) | Quantiles 0.487% / 2.047% | The levels at which the shipped constants sit |
| A-nom | 2% / 5% | The documented nominal rule |
| A-frozen | The shipped constants, not LOO | Reference |
| Curve | Six levels from 0.25% to 10% | Reported; no point selected from it |

**Decision rule.** `excluded = (B13 without evidence) OR corroboration`.

This decomposition was checked against direct `apply_defense("B13")` calls on owner 5:
- 60/60 identical to the direct call;
- 60/60 identical to the components computed with evidence.

The recomputed corroboration at the frozen constants reproduces the cached published components **item for item** on HO4, HO7 and HO8.

**Uncertainty.**
- Per-owner Wilson intervals.
- Pooled rates with an **owner-cluster bootstrap**: 10,000 resamples of owners, seed 2026.
- With 10 owners, an interval describes these owners. It is **not** a guarantee for an unseen population of owners.

### 3.1 Threshold stability

- **LOO thresholds, calibrated on nine owners, are stable.**
  - The exclude threshold ranges from 0.00189 to 0.00216, a max/min of **1.15** (prediction L1 ≤ 1.5: **HELD**).
  - The flag threshold ranges from 0.00528 to 0.00652.
- **Single-owner calibration is not stable** (descriptive).
  - Calibrating on one owner gives exclude thresholds from 0.00126 (owner 4) to 0.00550 (owner 5), a factor of **4.4**.
  - So the instability is real at **one calibration owner** and disappears when nine are pooled.
  - Practical implication: a deployment should not calibrate on a single user's history.

**Predictions:**
- **L4 HELD.** Wrong-owner benign exclusion is 31.6× the correct-owner rate (prediction ≥ 3×).

**Reading.** B13's cost is governed by the **quality of the evidence**, not the calibration owners.
- **Wrong or partial evidence** makes corroboration exclude large numbers of benign memories. On these populations it also detects *more* attacks under truncation, because everything looks unsupported. More exclusion here is not better security.
- **No evidence** turns corroboration off entirely. B13 then equals its non-corroboration components, which catch **0/200** fabrications here. B12 also catches 0/200.

## 5. Benign-exclusion cost depends on the benign population, not on calibration

| Benign population | A-ship benign exclusion |
|---|---|
| LoCoMo's own derived memories (grounded in the conversation by construction) | 0.59% pooled |
| HO4's ordinary notes (calendar, receipts, new facts the conversation never mentions) | **12.6%** (33/262) |
| HO7 | 18.0% (34/189) |

The same thresholds give these very different costs. The round-7 diagnosis stands: a benign new fact and a fabricated new fact are indistinguishable to corroboration. **The published "12–15% benign exclusion" for B13 is a property of ungrounded benign notes, not of calibration.**

| Policy | QA items that lose all linked memories |
|---|---|

**U2: task completion on affected QA items** (pre-registered as secondary; `b13_loo_utility.json`).

- **Sample.** All 55 QA items whose linked memories are excluded by at least one of A-ship, A-nom or B. That is 55 of 1,307, because the cap of 150 was not reached.
- **Setup.** Agent qwen2.5:7b; judge llama3.1:8b against the gold answer.
- **Scope.** This is task completion **on the affected items only**. It is not a population rate.

| Store | Correct (of 55) | vs no defence (paired) | QA items with all linked memories removed |
|---|---|---|---|
| No defence | 34 | — | 0 |
| **A-ship** | **29** | −5 net (6 lost, 1 gained), p = 0.125 | 7 |

Across all 1,307 linked QA items, A-ship's task cost is therefore bounded by these 55 items. That is at most about 6 items (0.5%) on this benign population. A-nom costs about four times as much.

## 8. Limitations

1. **Ten owners** from one dataset with one conversation style. The owner-bootstrap interval is conditional on these owners.
2. The fabrications are LLM-generated, with a generator × split confound.
3. Every population has been used before (pre-registration §0).
4. "Wrong owner" is one mismatch per owner, (o+1) mod 10. It is not a model of every evidence-poisoning adversary. An adversary who can write to the evidence store is outside this analysis.
5. Truncation is "first k% of turns", one form of incompleteness.
6. Owner-cluster bootstrap with 10 clusters gives approximate intervals.
